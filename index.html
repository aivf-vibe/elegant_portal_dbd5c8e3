Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our services

How long does a typical project take?

Project timelines vary based on complexity and scope. A simple website typically takes 2-4 weeks, while more complex projects like e-commerce platforms may take 6-12 weeks. We'll provide a detailed timeline during our initial consultation.

What is your pricing structure?

We offer flexible pricing options including fixed project rates, monthly retainers, and hourly consulting. Prices start from $2,500 for basic websites and scale based on features and complexity. Contact us for a personalized quote.

Do you provide ongoing support?

Yes! All our projects include 30 days of free support. We also offer ongoing maintenance packages starting at $99/month, which include updates, security patches, and technical support to keep your website running smoothly.

Can you help with website content?

Absolutely! We have experienced content strategists and copywriters who can help create compelling content that aligns with your brand voice and engages your target audience. Content creation services are available as an add-on to any project.
